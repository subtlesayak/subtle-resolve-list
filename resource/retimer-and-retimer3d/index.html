Retimer and Retimer3D

Retimes Fusion footage or 3D scenes by accumulating frame steps. Provides nearest, blend and existing-subframe modes; it does not generate new subframes. Manifest version 1.5 has a September 2026 package date, while its embedded 1.5 changelog is dated December 2023.
