fcp2aaf

Converts Final Cut FCPXML to audio AAF for Resolve/Fairlight and other audio applications. Runs locally on Apple Silicon with macOS 14+. Free conversions are limited to five minutes; paid licenses periodically need an online check. Version 26.9.21 refines the UI and keeps excluded tracks out of the preview.
